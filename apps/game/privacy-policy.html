Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

At Breakmine (breakmine.com), we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled across our main website, game services, wiki (wiki.breakmine.com), and modding platform (mods.breakmine.com).

We Do Not Sell Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties. Our wiki and modding platforms do not monetize your personal information.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Breakmine, we may collect minimal data necessary to provide our services:

  • Account Information: If you register an account on the wiki or mod repository, we store your chosen username and credentials (stored securely).
  • Content Contributions: Modifications, wiki edits, comments, and uploads you choose to share publicly.
  • Technical Logs: Standard server logs (such as IP addresses, browser types, and access timestamps) used strictly for security, abuse prevention, and basic site functionality.

How We Use Information

The information we collect is used solely for:

  • Operating and maintaining breakmine.com, wiki.breakmine.com, and mods.breakmine.com.
  • Allowing you to log in, upload mods, and edit wiki pages.
  • Protecting the platform against spam, abuse, and security threats.

Cookies

We may use essential session cookies strictly required for user authentication and maintaining your logged-in state on the wiki and modding platforms. We do not use tracking cookies for targeted advertising.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your account details and contributions. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@breakmine.com.
